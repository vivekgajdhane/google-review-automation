# Sales & Pilot Acquisition Plan: RCAFT

**Market:** Pune & Pimpri-Chinchwad (Baner, Wakad, Aundh, Koregaon Park)  
**Initial Pilot Target:** 5–10 Independent Premium Salons & Automotive Detailers  
**Pilot Offer:** ₹499/month (Includes 1 physical counter stand + dynamic QR + analytics + private feedback triage)  
**Date:** October 15, 2026  

---

## 1. Target Beachhead Persona & Criteria

- **Business Profile:** Independent, single-location or 2-branch salon, spa, or car detailing studio.
- **Location:** High footfall urban neighborhoods in Pune (Balewadi High Street, Baner Road, IT Park Wakad, North Main Road KP).
- **Owner Profile:** On-premises owner-operator or active manager who spends at least 3 days a week at the counter.
- **Review Status:** Currently has between 50 and 300 reviews on Google Maps with a rating between 4.1 and 4.7 stars (motivated to grow, but conscious of negative reviews).

## 3. The 5-Minute In-Person Demo Script

1. **Minute 1 — The Hook & Empathy (Counter Inspection):**
   - Look at their current counter setup. If they have a printed paper QR code or nothing:
   - *"When a client pays at your UPI scanner, how often do your stylists ask them for a Google review? What usually happens?"*
2. **Minute 2 — The Demonstration (Physical Scan):**
   - Hand the owner your sample acrylic counter stand.
   - *"Scan this with your regular phone camera right now."*
   - Let them experience the fast mobile page loading in under 1 second.
3. **Minute 3 — The Two-Choice Value Proposition:**
   - Point out the two clear buttons:
   - *"Notice two things: First, a happy client taps 'Open Google Review' and lands directly on your review box without searching. Second, if a client had an issue with wait time or staff behavior, they tap 'Send Private Feedback'. That message goes directly to your private dashboard instead of becoming a permanent 1-star scar on Google Maps."*
4. **Minute 4 — Truthful Analytics & Dynamic Protection:**
   - Show the dashboard on your tablet or phone:
   - *"If you ever change your Google link or branch name, this physical stand never goes to waste—we update the destination online in 5 seconds. Plus, you see exactly how many people scanned every week."*
5. **Minute 5 — The Pilot Close:**
   - *"We are onboarding 5 salons in Baner this week for our early-adopter pilot. For ₹499/month, we custom-print your counter stand with your logo, set up your dashboard, and give you weekly reports. If it doesn’t bring you cleaner reviews in 30 days, cancel anytime with zero lock-in. Can I print your stand for Monday?"*

| Objection | Context | Winning Response |

RCAFT is 100% policy-compliant and protects your Google Business listing."* |
| *"Why monthly? Can I just buy the stand once for ₹300?"* | Desire for one-off purchase | *"A static ₹300 stand gives you zero tracking and no private feedback inbox. Our ₹499/month service is a live software service: dynamic link updates, private feedback alerts, scan analytics, and replacement stands if yours gets damaged."* |

For each pilot location, track weekly:
- Total QR Scans / Page Visits
- Outbound Google Link Clicks
