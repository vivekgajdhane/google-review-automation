# Customer Validation Plan & Interview Script: RCAFT

**Author:** Student Founder & Senior Product Team  
**Geography:** Pune & Pimpri-Chinchwad (Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Wakad, Koregaon Park)  
**Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Target Sample:** 10–15 Local Business Owners (7 Salons/Spas, 4 Cafes, 4 Auto Detailers)  

---

## 1. Validation Objectives & Hypotheses

Before writing production application code or committing capital, we must validate three foundational hypotheses:
1. **Hypothesis 1 (Pain Intensity):** Local business owners actively worry about their Google Maps reviews and feel they miss out on reviews from happy customers who simply forget.
2. **Hypothesis 2 (Private Feedback Desirability):** Business owners value receiving discreet private complaints at checkout because it prevents damaging public 1-star reviews.
3. **Hypothesis 3 (Willingness to Pay):** Owners are willing to pay ₹499/month for dynamic link management, counter QR displays, and honest analytics, rather than relying on a static ₹300 acrylic stand.

---

## 2. Target Owner Interview Script (15–20 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Warm-up & Current Behavior (5 mins)
- *"Hi [Name], thank you for taking 15 minutes. I’m a student founder in Pune working on digital tools for local businesses. How long have you been operating this branch here in [Baner/Wakad]?"*
- *"How do new customers usually find you? What percentage mention Google Maps or local search?"*
- *"When a customer has a great service today, how do you ask them to leave a Google review? Who handles that?"*

### Phase 2: Uncovering Pain & Friction (5 mins)
- *"Do you currently display a QR code or standee at the billing counter? If yes, what kind?"*
- *"What is the most frustrating thing about collecting Google reviews? (e.g., customers say yes but forget, staff forget to ask, bad reviews from minor issues)?"*
- *"Has a customer ever left a 1-star or 2-star review on Google for something small that you could have easily fixed if they had told you on the spot (like AC temperature or waiting time)?"*

### Phase 3: Testing the RCAFT Concept (Without Leading) (5 mins)
- *"If you had a dynamic counter stand where a customer scans and gets two clear choices: tap to open your official Google review page immediately, OR send a direct private message straight to your personal phone/dashboard—how would that fit into your checkout?"*
- *"How important is it for you to see how many people actually scanned the stand every week versus how many tapped through to Google?"*
- *"Some software promises to block bad reviews or auto-generate fake reviews. Were you aware that Google actively penalizes and deletes listings that use 'review gating' or incentives?"*

### Phase 4: Price & Pilot Commitment (5 mins)
- *"We are running a 4-week pilot in Baner and Wakad with 5 selected salons. We provide the custom-designed counter stand, dynamic QR, private feedback dashboard, and weekly scan reports for ₹499/month with no lock-in. Would you be open to testing this at your reception desk next week?"*

## 3. Validation Metrics & Go / No-Go Decision Framework

| Metric | Target Goal | Go / Proceed Threshold | No-Go / Pivot Threshold |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Problem Resonance** | ≥ 70% | ≥ 10 of 15 owners cite Google review friction and desire for private feedback unprompted. | < 6 of 15 owners care about review collection or scan tracking. |
| **Existing QR / Standee Usage** | ≥ 60% | ≥ 9 of 15 already have some QR on counter (validates customer scan habit). | < 4 of 15 have QR (indicates low scan adoption in niche). |
| **Pilot Conversion Rate** | ≥ 30% | ≥ 4 of 15 owners agree to install the pilot counter stand and test the MVP for 4 weeks. | < 2 owners willing to place the stand on their counter. |
| **Willingness to Pay (₹499/mo)** | ≥ 25% | ≥ 3 of 15 commit to paid pilot or express immediate purchase intent upon seeing demo. | 0 owners willing to pay more than a one-time ₹200 standee fee. |

---

## 4. Go / No-Go Decision Triggers

- **GREEN LIGHT (Continue to MVP):** If ≥ 4 owners agree to the 4-week pilot and confirm that private feedback triage + dynamic QR analytics solve a real headache.
- **AMBER LIGHT (Refine Proposition):** If owners want the stand but resist monthly SaaS pricing; pivot model to **₹1,200 one-time setup (includes physical acrylic stand + 1 year hosted dynamic link)**.
- **RED LIGHT (Halt / Pivot):** If owners report that customers refuse to scan counter QR codes or if Google reviews are irrelevant to their customer acquisition.
