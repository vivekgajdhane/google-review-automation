# Product Brief: RCAFT

**Working Product Name:** RCAFT (Review & Customer Authentic Feedback Tool)  
**Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Status:** Stage A Product Validation  
**Target Region:** Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and Western Maharashtra, India  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

RCAFT is a lightweight, mobile-first, ethical QR customer feedback and Google review-link assistant built specifically for local independent businesses in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC). 

Unlike legacy enterprise reputation software (Birdeye, Podium) that costs ₹25,000–₹35,000/month or questionable "review booster" tools that violate Google's policies via review gating and fake sentiment routing, RCAFT provides a 100% Google-policy-compliant, high-trust workflow:
1. A single dynamic QR code placed on physical billing counters, tables, or takeaway packaging.
2. A fast, clean, mobile-first public page presenting **two equal, unconditioned choices**:
   - **"Open Google Review Page"** (direct link to the official Google listing).
   - **"Send Private Feedback"** (direct, private communication channel to the business owner).
3. An optional, strictly constrained, customer-controlled AI wording clarity aid that assists customers in phrasing their own voluntary feedback for grammar/readability without hallucinating facts or altering sentiment.
4. An honest, privacy-preserving business dashboard tracking scans, feedback starts, submissions, and Google review link clicks—**never falsely claiming that a public review was published**.

## 2. Target Audience & Niche Analysis

### Primary Geographic Focus
- Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC): Baner, Aundh, Kothrud, Viman Nagar, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, FC Road.
- Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC): Wakad, Pimple Saudagar, Ravet, Hinjawadi tech corridor.

### Initial Beachhead Segment Recommendation
**Recommended First Niche:** **Independent Premium Salons, Spas & Beauty Studios**
* **Rationale:**
  - High repeat interaction & personal relationship between service provider and client.
  - Customer dwells at billing counter or waiting lounge for 3–5 minutes after service.
  - High sensitivity to Google ratings (a 4.6 vs 4.1 star rating makes or breaks new client acquisition in Baner/Wakad).
  - Owners are on-site or easily accessible compared to multi-franchise owners.
  - Lower regulatory/privacy risk than healthcare/clinics.
  - Urgent need for private operational feedback (e.g., cleanliness, wait times, AC temperature) without public drama.
* **Secondary Cohorts for Pilot Testing:**
  - Specialty Cafes & Casual Dining (Boutique eateries with loyal clientele).
  - Multi-brand Automobile Detailers & Two-Wheeler Service Centers.

## 3. Problem Hypothesis

### The Customer Problem:
- Willing customers often abandon leaving a review because search friction is high ("Which Baner branch is this?"), they are in a rush, or they find typing a detailed description tedious on mobile.
- Dissatisfied customers who just wanted an operational issue fixed (e.g., slow service or billing error) post a 1-star public Google review because the business offered **no direct, friction-free private feedback channel** at checkout.

### The Business Owner Problem:
- Static printed Google QR codes break if the link structure changes or the listing URL is updated, requiring expensive reprints.
- Google provides zero analytics on how many people scanned a counter stand versus how many opened the link.
- Aggressive review platforms sell "Review Shield / Gating" which violates Google's User Contributed Content policies and risks merchant penalties, listing suspension, or Google Consumer Warning badges.
- Enterprise software costs hundreds of dollars per month with long annual lock-ins, complex onboarding, and foreign USD pricing incompatible with local Indian SME budgets.

## 4. Value Proposition

> **For Local Business Owners:** "Get an elegant, dynamic QR counter display and honest analytics that makes it effortless for happy customers to reach your official Google review page, while giving every customer a direct private channel to voice concerns before they leave."

> **For End Customers:** "Scan once, get instant access to the official Google review page or share private feedback directly with management in 30 seconds—no app download, no login required."

---

## 5. Non-Goals (What RCAFT Is NOT)

1. **NOT a Review-Gating Engine:** RCAFT will never filter, hide, or delay the Google review link based on customer rating, star selection, or negative sentiment.
2. **NOT an Auto-Poster:** RCAFT will never access customer Google credentials, scrape Google, bypass CAPTCHAs, or publish reviews automatically. Google does not permit automated consumer review submission.
3. **NOT an Incentive Scheme:** No coupons, discounts, prize draws, or perks in exchange for public Google reviews.
4. **NOT a Fake Review Generator:** RCAFT does not invent reviews, hallucinate customer experiences, or inject SEO keywords.
5. **NOT an Enterprise CRM:** No multi-tier enterprise permissions, omnichannel ticketing, or complex POS integrations in MVP.
